Novae Data Protection Notice

Date2026-08-06

Overview

This Data Protection Notice explains how Novae collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the app. Novae is a singing practice app focused on recording or importing vocal practice audio, extracting backing tracks from video or audio files, enhancing vocal recordings, exporting practice results, and optionally generating AI-based practice feedback. This notice applies to the Novae app distributed through the Apple App Store.

Novae is designed to keep practice records primarily on your device. Your song details, practice notes, lyric notes, tags, self-ratings, imported files, recorded audio, processed vocal files, and viewing history are generally stored locally on your device. Audio is not uploaded by default. Upload occurs only when you actively request an AI review feature.

User Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or export certain personal information associated with your use of Novae. Because most practice data is stored locally on your device, you can directly edit or delete many records within the app.

Access: You may review your practice history, notes, and saved files in the app.

Correction: You may update song titles, origin labels, keys, moods, tags, notes, and self-ratings within the app.

Deletion: You may delete local audio, waveform cache, review history, and practice records from the app settings or individual record views.

Export: You may export your processed practice files using the app's save or sharing features.

Withdraw consent: You may stop using optional permissions, such as microphone access, at any time through your device settings. You may also stop using the AI review feature at any time.

Contact: If you have questions about privacy rights, you may use the contact method listed in the Contact Information section below.

International Data Transfers

Novae may process limited service-related data in different regions depending on where infrastructure systems are located or where users access the service. If you choose to request AI review or use account-related online functions, related processing may occur on servers located outside your country or region.

When cross-border processing occurs, we take reasonable measures intended to protect information in line with applicable legal requirements. Regardless of region, the principles described in this notice apply to the handling of your data.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. These measures may include encrypted transmission where appropriate, access controls, restricted internal access, secure storage practices, and verification steps for purchase-related records.

Local practice content is primarily stored on your device. You are also responsible for protecting your device, operating system, and account environment. No security method is perfect, and we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to reduce risk and limit unnecessary data collection.

AI Processing

Novae may offer optional AI-generated singing practice feedback when you explicitly request it. We do NOT use any third-party AI services to process user data. All AI processing is performed exclusively on our own internal servers.

When you choose AI review, only the content necessary to generate the requested feedback is processed. Uploaded audio used for AI processing is deleted after processing is completed, except where a limited stored result is needed to provide the review outcome shown in your history. Temporary processing data is deleted after completion. We do NOT use your audio, notes, metadata, or generated results for model training, model tuning, or model evaluation. AI processing in Novae is only for delivering the feature you asked for.

Information We Collect

We collect information in the following categories:

Information you provide directly: song title, origin status such as Original or Cover, key, mood, technique focus tags, lyric notes, self-ratings, feedback requests, support messages, and any files you record or import, including audio and video selected for backing-track extraction.

Audio and media content: vocal recordings, imported local audio, imported local video used for audio extraction, processed vocal exports, waveform previews, and optional AI review inputs and outputs.

Purchase and transaction information: coin balance records, purchase status, and encrypted purchase verification information related to Apple in-app purchases.

Automatically collected information: basic technical information such as app version, device type, operating system version, error diagnostics, and limited usage events needed to operate features, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot issues.

Permissions data: microphone access is requested only when you choose to record a singing practice take.

Cookies and similar technologies: The mobile app does not rely on browser cookies in the ordinary sense. If limited identifiers or similar local technologies are used, they are used only for app operation, session continuity, security, and feature delivery, not for advertising or profiling.

Children's Privacy

Novae is intended for users aged 17 and older. We do not knowingly target or knowingly collect personal information from children under 17. If we become aware that information from a person under 17 has been collected contrary to this policy, we will take reasonable steps to delete it as appropriate.

Data Retention

We retain data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this notice, unless a longer period is required by law.

Local practice content: Practice records, imported files, recordings, processed outputs, notes, tags, and waveform previews remain on your device until you delete them.

Uploaded content for AI processing: Uploaded content is deleted after processing.

Generated AI review results: Generated results may be stored in your in-app history so you can review them later, and you can delete them at any time from the app.

Temporary processing data: Temporary processing data is deleted after completion.

Crash logs and diagnostics: Retained for up to 12 months, unless a shorter retention period is operationally sufficient.

Support messages and feedback communications: Retained for up to 6 months unless longer retention is necessary to resolve an ongoing issue or comply with legal obligations.

Purchase-related records: Retained for the period reasonably necessary for transaction verification, fraud prevention, accounting, and legal compliance.

Changes to Policy

We may update this Data Protection Notice from time to time to reflect product changes, legal requirements, or improvements in our practices. When we make material changes, we may revise the effective date and provide notice within the app or through other appropriate means. Your continued use of Novae after the updated notice becomes effective indicates acceptance of the revised terms to the extent permitted by law.

How We Use Information

We use information only for legitimate app functions and service delivery, including:

recording and managing singing practice sessions;

importing audio or video files and extracting backing tracks;

processing vocal audio with user-selected enhancement settings;

saving, exporting, and sharing practice outputs at your direction;

showing history, waveforms, tags, and progress records;

providing optional AI review when you request it;

processing purchases, coin deductions, and free export usage;

maintaining app functionality, security, and support.

We do NOT use your information for AI training. We do NOT use your information for advertising. We do NOT use your information for behavioral profiling. We do NOT sell your personal information or your user content.

Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade user data. We do NOT share any user-uploaded or user-generated content with any third party. We do NOT use any third-party AI services to process user data. All AI processing is performed exclusively on our own internal servers.

Where limited third-party services are used, they are used only for cloud storage, server hosting, or basic infrastructure operations. Any such providers act solely on our behalf and under our instructions, are bound by contractual obligations to ensure data security, and are NOT permitted to use user data for any purpose beyond what we instruct.

These infrastructure providers do NOT use data for advertising or user profiling. They do NOT use data for AI training or model optimization. They do NOT use data for their own purposes or redistribute it. They do NOT independently access or use user content except as technically necessary to provide contracted infrastructure services under our control.

We may also disclose limited information if required by applicable law, regulation, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security, but we do not voluntarily disclose user content for unrelated third-party purposes.

Face Data / Biometric Information

Novae does not provide facial recognition features and does not perform biometric analysis. We do not create face templates, voiceprints for identity recognition, biometric identifiers, or biometric profiles. The app is focused on singing practice audio processing, not identity analysis.

If any media file selected by a user incidentally contains a person's image or voice, Novae does not use that content for face identification, biometric matching, or biometric tracking. Temporary processing data is deleted after completion, and no biometric database is created.

User Consent and Acceptance

By downloading, accessing, or using Novae, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Data Protection Notice and agree to the collection, use, storage, and handling of information as described here. If you do not agree with this notice, please do not use the app.
