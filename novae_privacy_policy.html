Privacy Notice | Novae

Effective: 2026-08-05

Part 1 – Overview

Novae is a singing practice app focused on recording vocals, importing local audio or video files, extracting backing tracks, enhancing vocal recordings, adding room-style reverb, exporting practice results, and keeping a personal practice history. This Privacy Notice explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices available to you.

Novae is designed to keep core singing practice content on your device whenever possible. Practice records such as song title, origin selection, key, mood, technique tags, lyric notes, self-ratings, waveforms, original recordings, processed vocal results, and export history are primarily stored locally on your device. We do not upload your singing recordings or practice history to our servers for general storage or sharing.

Please read this Privacy Notice carefully before using the app. If you do not agree with this notice, please do not use Novae.

Part 2 – Children's Privacy

Novae is intended for users aged 17 and older. The app is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 17. If we become aware that information has been provided by a person under 17 in violation of this policy, we will take reasonable steps to delete that information as appropriate.

Part 3 – Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. These measures may include encrypted storage where appropriate, secure transmission methods, access controls, limited internal access, and monitoring for service integrity and abuse prevention.

Audio processing outputs, purchase-related records, and account-related technical data are handled with security safeguards appropriate to the nature of the data. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You are also responsible for protecting your own device, system account access, and any exported files you choose to share through your device or third-party platforms.

Part 4 – Changes to Policy

We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time to reflect app changes, legal requirements, or operational needs. When we make material changes, we may revise the effective date above and provide notice within the app or through other appropriate means. Your continued use of Novae after an update takes effect means you accept the revised Privacy Notice.

Part 5 – Face Data / Biometric Information

Novae does not use facial recognition. Novae does not perform biometric identification or biometric analysis. Novae does not create, store, collect, or share face templates, face geometry, voiceprints for identity matching, or any other biometric identifiers.

The app is focused on singing practice audio and does not require face scanning, identity verification by face, or biometric profiling. If any temporary media file is processed by the app for user-requested functionality, it is not used for facial recognition or biometric purposes, and any temporary processing data is deleted after processing is completed.

Part 6 – AI Processing

Novae does not use any third-party AI services to process user data. All AI processing is performed exclusively on our own internal servers.

If any AI-assisted or automated processing is introduced for internal product functions, it will occur only on our own servers and only for the purpose of providing the requested app feature. We do not send user content to third-party AI providers, model APIs, or external AI platforms.

User data processed by any internal AI or automated system is not used for model training, model fine-tuning, or model optimization. Temporary processing data is deleted after processing is completed. We do not retain user content for AI training purposes.

At the product level, Novae mainly relies on device-based and controlled audio processing workflows for vocal enhancement, noise reduction, reverb effects, and format conversion. Those functions are not used to build user profiles or train AI systems.

Part 7 – How We Use Information

We use information only for legitimate app operations and user-requested features, including:

  • Recording and managing singing practice sessions.
  • Importing local audio or video files selected by the user.
  • Extracting backing tracks from imported media.
  • Processing vocals with selected enhancement and reverb settings.
  • Saving practice history, notes, tags, and related metadata on the device.
  • Supporting exports, sharing actions, and file management requested by the user.
  • Managing coin balance, export deductions, purchase validation, and transaction records.
  • Providing customer support, troubleshooting, and app reliability improvements.
  • Maintaining security, preventing abuse, and meeting legal obligations.

We do NOT use user data for AI training. We do NOT use user data for advertising. We do NOT use user data for profiling. We do NOT sell user data.

Part 8 – Information We Collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Information you provide directly: song title, origin selection, key, mood, technique focus tags, lyric notes, self-ratings, feedback messages, support requests, and other content you choose to enter.
  • Audio and media selected by you: vocal recordings you create in the app, audio files you import, video files you import for backing-track extraction, processed vocal outputs, and waveform previews generated from your files.
  • Purchase and store information: coin purchases, export consumption records, transaction validation data, and limited purchase-related technical records needed to support Apple in-app purchases.
  • Device and technical information: app version, device model, operating system version, language setting, diagnostic events, crash information, and similar technical data necessary for security and performance.
  • Permission-related data: microphone access status when you choose to record audio.
  • Cookies or similar technologies: Novae does not operate as a web advertising platform. If limited similar technologies are used in related support systems or infrastructure, they are used only for basic functionality, security, or session management, not for advertising or profiling.

Novae does not require camera access or photo library access for its intended functionality. Local media import is handled through system file selection when you choose to import files.

Part 9 – Data Sharing

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade user data.

We do NOT share any user-uploaded or user-generated content with any third party.

We do NOT use any third-party AI services to process user data. All AI processing is performed exclusively on our own internal servers.

Where third-party services are used at all, they are limited to basic infrastructure operations such as cloud storage, server hosting, or essential service delivery support. Any such provider acts solely on our behalf and under our instructions, is bound by contractual obligations to ensure data security, and is NOT permitted to use user data for any purpose beyond what we instruct.

These third parties do NOT use data for advertising or user profiling. They do NOT use data for AI training or model optimization. They do NOT use data for their own purposes or redistribute it. They do NOT independently access or use user content except as strictly necessary to provide infrastructure services under our instructions.

We may also disclose limited information if required by law, regulation, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, security, or the integrity of the app. In such cases, we limit disclosure to what is reasonably necessary.

Part 10 – International Data Transfers

Depending on your location and the technical structure of our services, limited operational data may be processed in different countries or regions where we or our infrastructure providers operate. When such transfers occur, we take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place consistent with applicable law.

Because Novae is primarily designed for local storage of practice content, many user files remain on the user’s device. If any service-related data is processed across regions, it will be handled only for legitimate operational purposes and subject to security controls.

Part 11 – User Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to:

  • Request access to information associated with you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of eligible information.
  • Request export of certain information where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent for permissions such as microphone access by changing your device settings.
  • Delete local practice records, local audio files, and waveform cache from within the app settings where available.

You may also manage app permissions directly in your device settings. If you want to exercise privacy rights or make a privacy-related request, you may contact us using the contact method listed below.

Part 12 – Data Retention

We retain data only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Notice.

  • Uploaded or imported content used for processing: deleted after processing when retained only as temporary processing input, unless you choose to keep the local file on your device.
  • Temporary processing data: deleted after completion of processing.
  • Generated results: stored in your local practice history until you delete them; you can delete them at any time through the app or by removing app data from your device.
  • Practice metadata and history: stored locally on your device until you delete it.
  • Crash logs and diagnostics: retained for up to 12 months.
  • Support messages and feedback submissions: retained for up to 6 months, unless a longer period is required for legal or dispute resolution purposes.
  • Purchase-related records: retained as needed for accounting, fraud prevention, legal compliance, and transaction support.

Part 13 – User Consent and Acceptance

By downloading, accessing, or using Novae, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Notice and agree to the collection, use, storage, and handling of information as described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.
