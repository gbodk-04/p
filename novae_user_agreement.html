Article VI: Description of the Service

Novae is a singing-practice-focused audio tool designed to help users record or import vocal practice takes, extract MP3 backing audio from supported video or audio sources, apply controlled vocal cleanup and enhancement, add room-style ambience and reverb, and export a practice vocal result for replay or sharing. The app is not offered as a general-purpose audio workstation, full-featured editing suite, or universal media converter.

The service centers on a structured singing practice workflow. A user may start a new practice session, record a vocal take or import a vocal file, optionally select or create a reusable local backing track reference, preview the original waveform, choose lightweight processing controls such as noise reduction, vocal enhancement, and reverb level, and then preview the processed result before exporting. Users may also add song title, origin classification such as Original or Cover, key, mood, technique focus tags, lyric notes, and self-evaluation details to preserve a practice log over time.

Novae supports local import of certain audio and video files and may extract an audio track from a video into MP3 format for use as a backing reference. Processed vocal output may be exported in supported audio formats such as m4a, and the app may generate recap information such as duration, sample rate, format, export status, waveform preview, and related practice fields. Backing materials are maintained separately from processed practice vocal results.

The app also includes history and review functions intended to help users revisit prior sessions, filter sessions by fields such as origin, key, technique focus, and export status, and observe personal practice patterns. Recording, importing, processing preview, and listening functions may be free, while successful export or save actions may require free export credits or virtual coins obtained through in-app purchase. Features, supported file types, processing quality, export options, and pricing structures may change over time.

Novae is designed for local-device use. Based on the current service design, practice records, notes, original audio, processed results, and related metadata are intended to remain stored on the user's device unless the user chooses to share or export them. The app may request microphone access only when needed for recording. It does not require camera access for its core functions, and imported media may be selected from device files rather than from direct photo library integration.

Novae does not promise professional vocal coaching, competition scoring, pitch certification, rhythm grading, or guaranteed improvement. Any enhancement, ambience effect, export result, or progress view is provided only as a practice aid and listening tool.
