Part 10 – Description of the Service

Novae is a singing practice-focused audio processing and record-keeping application designed for users who want to practice vocals, review progress, and receive lightweight automated feedback. The service is not presented as a general-purpose audio editor. Its core purpose is to support a practice loop of recording or importing a vocal take, listening back, optionally receiving AI commentary, and practicing again.

The app allows users to record vocal takes through microphone access or import local audio and video files from device storage. Supported user flows may include importing a video to extract a backing track in MP3 format, importing a vocal recording, or recording a new take while referencing a local accompaniment source. Each practice entry may include structured practice fields such as song title, whether the piece is an Original or Cover, key, mood, technique focus tags, lyric notes, and self-evaluation details.

Novae includes controlled vocal processing tools tailored to singing practice. These may include low-frequency cleanup, optional gentle noise reduction, optional vocal enhancement, limiting, and room-style ambience created through reverb processing intended to simulate a karaoke or practice-room feel. Users may preview original and processed audio, view waveform-based summaries, and export a processed practice vocal for saving or sharing where supported by the device.

The app also keeps local practice history so users can review prior sessions and track development over time. History may display fields such as date, song title, origin, key, mood, technique focus tags, self-rating, duration details, reverb level, format, sample rate, export status, and listening activity. Practice records are intended to help users reflect on progress rather than provide professional performance analytics.

Novae may provide an optional AI review feature known as Aria AI. When you actively request that feature, the selected recording may be uploaded for automated analysis and the system may return a reference score and written feedback. Such feedback is intended to provide general encouragement and practice suggestions only. It is not a professional vocal judgment, pitch certification, rhythm grading, or competition evaluation.

Most practice creation features are intended to remain free to use, including recording, importing, practice logging, backing track extraction, processing previews, and review of previously generated AI feedback. Charges may apply only to certain successful actions, such as exporting a processed practice vocal or generating a new AI review, as further described in the in-app purchases section below.

Novae is also designed with a local-first model for practice records. Practice metadata, notes, recordings, processed outputs, and AI review text may be stored on the device. Recordings are not uploaded by default. Upload occurs only when you intentionally request cloud-based AI review. The app may further offer settings for account actions, data cleanup, access to terms and privacy documents, feedback channels, and coin purchase options.
