Section H – Description of the Service

Havki is a mobile application that helps users create emotionally matched quote cards from photos. You may upload a scene photo or capture one with your camera, and the app will analyze the image to identify broad scene characteristics such as location, time, mood, atmosphere, subjects, actions, and color tone.

Based on that analysis, Havki generates three short quote cards, each paired with a brief interpretation and designed for sharing, copying, or saving. Users may optionally choose among styles such as Healing, Motivational, Reflective, and Stoic. The app also includes local history management, image-based card sharing, clipboard copying, and a simple coin-based access model with a small number of free initial uses.

The service may request camera or photo library access only when needed for uploading, capturing, or saving content. Uploaded images are processed for AI analysis, and the service is designed to minimize retained data. Havki may also display a notice that generated quotes may not reflect factual accuracy.
